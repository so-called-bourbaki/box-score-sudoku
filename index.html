New to scorekeeping? Read the short guide

Runners left means runners still on base when the inning ends. Brooklyn left 7; Cleveland left 6. Split each team’s total across its innings, from 0 to 3 per inning.

For this game, batters = 3 outs + runs + runners left. The batting order continues where the previous inning stopped. That is why earlier innings unlock later ones.

AB–H–R means at-bats, hits, runs. Compare “Your” totals with the printed “Box” totals. Walks and sacrifices do not count as at-bats. After a batter reaches base, tick Scored if he comes home.

Choose Out for a batter retired on a double or triple play; place the matching play card to count the runners retired too. “Safe on a force” counts one runner out automatically. Add extra runner outs only when they are not already counted.

What does the lab check?

It enumerates runners-left patterns using the line score, team totals, batting order, scoring assignments, sacrifices, and pitchers’ turns at bat. It does not place every hit or establish a complete legal sequence. A surviving pattern still needs to be worked out on the scorecard.

Investigate the triple play · Who could have been up?

Nobody out, two on, one play ends the inning: three batters, no runs, nobody left. The two runners were the two men just ahead of the batter.
